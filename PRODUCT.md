# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro (static output) deployed to Vercel. Chosen because the site is content-led (project pages, case studies) with little interactivity; Astro ships near-zero JS by default, makes per-project pages simple via content collections, and deploys to Vercel with no config.

## Users

Franz works both as a developer inside an agency and with his own freelance clients. Primary: business owners and decision-makers anywhere in the world (remote engagement) who need a WordPress or Shopify site built, rebuilt, or extended, and are evaluating whether to hire a freelance developer. They arrive comparing options, want proof of comparable work, and decide whether to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that wins freelance web development work. Success = a qualified prospect sends an enquiry after seeing real, relevant work.

## Positioning

A freelance web developer specialising in WordPress and Shopify, marketed under the developer's own name to a global client base. The specialism (these two platforms, not "any stack") is the core claim.

## Operating Context

- Prospects typically review live sites the developer built, then decide whether to make contact.
- Engagement is remote; nothing should imply a local-only service area.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Hosted on Vercel.
- Contact method: an enquiry form (endpoint still to be set). Pricing presentation is not decided; every job is quoted, so never invent prices.

## Brand Commitments

- Uses the developer's own name as the brand: **Franz Mozar** (confirmed 2026-10-29).
- Logo (chosen 2026-10-29 from four options): lowercase "franzmozar." wordmark in Archivo ExtraBold with a glowing mint full stop; compact icon is a drawn "fm." in a dark tile. Files: `src/components/Logo.astro`, `public/logo-mark.svg`, `public/favicon.svg`; usage sheet at `logo/logo-sheet.html`. The user rejected an earlier "FM" monogram tile as looking bad.
- Standing visual preference (user, 2026-10-29): dark, sleek, professional "tech" look (near-black, crisp grid, one bright accent, site-screenshot showcase), at the craft level of top dark product/studio sites. The user rejected a themed (estate-agent window) direction as not professional enough.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over claims: real, live sites carry the argument; copy stays short and specific.
2. Platform specialism is explicit: WordPress and Shopify are named, not hidden behind "web solutions".
3. Every page leads toward an enquiry.
4. The site itself is evidence of craft: fast, accessible, and well built.
